Personal Brand Statement

Teaching Physics. Inspiring Curiosity. Building Understanding.

Physics is not a collection of isolated equations to be memorized for exams—it is the foundational language of how our universe functions. By integrating conceptual reasoning, Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs), rigorous mathematical derivations, and visual problem-solving, we cultivate independent scientific thinkers ready for higher education and academic excellence.
